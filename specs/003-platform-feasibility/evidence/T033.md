## Learnings

Class: sensor.

- A fake that models "acknowledged but not effective" must apply it to every route the cleanup may use (DELETE and PUT), and a test of a cleanup's leftover check needs one case per kind left alone, not all kinds together. (round 1, round 2)
- A "no target" or blocked case must still require the work that should happen (controls and reads), or a check that skips everything and invents a result passes. (round 1)
- Variant loops in a test take `t.Run` per variant, so a `Fatalf` in one does not hide the next. (round 2)
- Prove a correct alternative passes (an `ALT-` row in the mutant script) when a test could be quietly requiring one implementation route. (round 2)
