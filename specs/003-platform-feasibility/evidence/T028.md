## Learnings

Class: guidance. A run's cleanup with `--cleanup` rewrites `summary.json` and `leftovers.json`, so
the first leftover check's errors survive only in the terminal log: the evidence has to quote the
log line, and the error detail is lost (T027, T028). A rounded bound in a record (`upper_seconds: 0`)
is recomputed from the timestamps before it is read as "immediate" (T026).
