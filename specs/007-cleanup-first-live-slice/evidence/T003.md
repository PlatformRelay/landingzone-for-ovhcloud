## Learnings

Class: guidance.
- A retry case on a fixture must count requests per page, not per path: a multi-page listing makes a correct retry send more requests than "attempts" (review round 1).
- A backoff fixture of equal values cannot tell the second wait from the first; give each wait its own value (review round 2).
