## What happened

- The probe's code was on `main` and independently reviewed: US3 (#136), T026 (#148), and the
  stage B fixes T027 (#163, `49e49ca`).
- Every probe start is refused while another probe run is unreconciled (FR-006). When the window
  plan was written on 2026-10-19, nine runs from before the baseline existed (spec 003, 2026-10-10
  and 2026-10-16) still held directories under `state/probes/` (`window.md` row 0, `retained.md`
  B1). They can be cleared only with `--cleanup <run-id> --accept-lost-baseline`,
  which needs the owner's terminal (FR-007; D124).
- The owner cleared six of them on 2026-10-19 and 2026-10-20. Three stayed: one `network` probe run
  of 2026-10-10 and two `swift-reach` runs of 2026-10-16.
- On 2026-10-20 at about 21:56 the coordinator ran a plain `--cleanup` of each of the three, without
  the flag. Each was refused `BASELINE_MISSING` (untrusted) before anything was read or deleted.
- The cut-off, 2026-10-21 23:00 (L6), passed with the three still open. T019 was not started.

## What this leaves

- Live retry behaviour of the alert sweep (T027) stays UNVERIFIED.
