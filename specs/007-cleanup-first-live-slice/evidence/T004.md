## Design of the cases (from spec.md FR-001/SC-001 and data-model.md *Fixed signatures*, not plan.md)

- **Fault.** `finalFault` wraps the chain's Lister seam (`chainExec.wrapLister`) and, once `chainExec.atFinal` marks the final reconciliation (the first listing after an apply), answers the first first-page request with `*statusError{500}` after the fake API served it. The path is whichever the check lists first (here `/cloud/project/<project>/region`, an array listing); the case reads it back from the wrapper.
- **Why a slow retained read (case 1).** spec 006's budget has state only in its reserve (spec 006 T024 lesson: a budget whose only state is its end cannot tell "shared" from "fresh"). The retained read's wait (4 min, admitted) spends the budget; its retry then takes 3 min on the run's clock, which `keep` makes the shared reserve. With 8 min 30 s left at the check's start, about 1 min 30 s remain at the listing: the shared budget (reserve ≥ 3 min) refuses the 5 s wait; a fresh `newRetryBudget(end, minRetryReserve)` (30 s) and a nil read as unbounded both admit it; the context's deadline (1 min 30 s away) admits it, so the refusal can only be the budget's (believed, arithmetic from `deadline.go:63-76` and `chain.go:665-690` at `449b046`, read 2026-10-16; margins ≥ 55 s against real time elapsed in the destroy phase).
- **Clock (case 2).** The listing's wait is asserted on the run's `retryClock` (data-model: the chain sets `check.Clock` to `r.stateReadClock`) and equal to `defaultListingBackoff[0]` (the chain sets no `Backoff`).
- **Probe (case 3).** `destroyClock` anchors the check's clock at the latest deadline a child ran under (seen through `Runner.childStarted`: the destroy context's, 45 min from the destroy's start, verified by a premise assertion), so the edge is exact: 5 s more than reserve + wait is admitted, 5 s less is refused. A probe core with no budget fails *admitted*; one ending at the run's 30 s deadline fails *admitted*; one unbounded fails *refused*.
- **Retry line (case 2).** FR-001 "each retry prints one line …": asserted on the chain's terminal, prefix `LZ-LIVE retry listing <path> in `.

## Learnings

Class: guidance.
- A wiring test for "the same budget" needs the shared budget to carry state the fresh one lacks: raise its reserve with a slow read *after* the spending wait, or the case only tests the end time (spec 006 T024's lesson, applied).
- An edge test against a deadline the code computes itself: anchor the fake clock at the deadline a child actually ran under (`childStarted`), not at the wall clock, and assert the anchor as a premise.
