## Learnings
- Class: spec. A list the task text says the coordinator builds from a live record cannot be closed by a subagent: it can derive the rows from code, but the record read and "no blocker open" stay the coordinator's. Proposed: split such a task into an agent part (rows from code) and a coordinator part (record read, blocker check), or dispatch it to the coordinator only.
- A removal order for an account must end with the identity that removes the rest, and that identity's own removal is the owner's in the Control Panel: deleting its policy first leaves it unable to delete itself.
