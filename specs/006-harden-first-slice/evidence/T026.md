## Learnings

Class: guidance.
- Before wiring a live read, check that the credential it uses is granted the action (the module's policy), not only that the fake admits the key: a fake that admits by bucket membership passes a read the real policy refuses.
- A coordinator's or an earlier task's note on how to observe an outcome does not override the requirement's wording; check the judge's mapping of the observed value against the spec's named outcome at the chain, with a test that reads `summary.json`.
