## Learnings

Class: sensor.
- A plan guard keyed on addresses must also judge `previous_address`: a `moved` block can take a retained resource away and delete or replace it in the same plan, or move it out of the set so a later plan deletes it unguarded.
- A guard reading `tofu show -json` must check it was given a plan (`planned_values`), not the state that `show -json` prints without a plan file; synthetic rows then need the plan marker, or every refuse row passes vacuously on the "not a plan" path.
- Judge actions against an allow-list of harmless actions and refuse an entry with no actions: unknown shapes fail closed.
- When a new check subsumes an older one's controls, rerun the mutants: the older clause's mutant survives until a row reaches it past the new check.
