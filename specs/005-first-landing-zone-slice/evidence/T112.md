## Learnings

- sensor: a fixture transplant from another capture should carry every field of the changed side (`before`, `before_sensitive`), or the test reads a hybrid the tool never writes.
- spec: "skip like a delete" makes a forget free its name for the uniqueness check although the bucket stays; if account-wide uniqueness matters, a follow-up reserves forgotten names (from `before.name`) instead of dropping them.
- tooling: `test:stacks`'s registry inputs omit `tests/fixtures/outputs` and `tests/fixtures/tofu-probes/protect`, which its tests read; fold into T111.
