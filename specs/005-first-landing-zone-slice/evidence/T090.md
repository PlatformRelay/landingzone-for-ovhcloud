4. **Locks**: held over the whole acted set, blocked stacks included (unchanged from T059; the same tenant/account locks in the fixture). A blocked consumer of a consumer is blocked on its own direct selected producer (transitive consumers are selected, R21).
5. **Selection under the locks**: the blocked map comes from the second selection (under the locks), as the acted set does.
